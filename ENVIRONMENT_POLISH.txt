ASHEN VOW - ENVIRONMENT POLISH / AUTHORING HANDOFF
4 October 2026

SCOPE
Presentation-only pass across Stillwood March. Scale is still 128 units/metre.
No changes to player/Raven movement, camera, stamina, combat, encounters,
physical routes, landmark transforms, collision footprints or navigation.
The existing Mobile renderer, lighting, spell feedback and particle budgets remain.

REGION AUDIT AND TREATMENT
Deep woodland: fern beds, moss, low shrubs, branches and layered leaf litter.
Pine woodland / Split Oak: needle-rich floor, roots, worn clearing and broken wood.
Gravewood / Rootbound Graves: damp moss, roots, disturbed litter and sparse stone;
  memorial posts frame the road without blocking it or regularising grave rows.
Stillwood camp: feathered soil edge, trampled shrine approach and lower cover;
  bare reservations protect shrine/fire/bedrolls/firewood. Their positions stay put.
Woodland edge: overlapping tree-family probabilities and mixed grass/fern/shrub
  patches replace the dominant-biome family switch. Existing tree coordinates,
  density, spacing and trunk radii are preserved.
Plains: larger varied grass/shrub clumps with stone and bare-earth breaks.
Fort heath: shorter, drier cover and more stone as the player approaches Greywatch.
Greywatch gate: worn widening approach, paired dark pennants, contrasting threshold,
  and broken spring stones instead of a solid-looking overhead gate slab. The
  actual gate opening, towers and all collision stay unchanged.
Greywatch interior: sparse stone/moss/weeds and a distinct route to the hall steps.
Chapel: pale worn approach, modest memorial framing, moss/litter-rich margins.
  Roof cutaway and existing building/interior collision systems remain intact.
Watchtower: rubble/gravel approach and low framing towards the accessible forecourt;
  no false doorway has been painted into the solid tower.
Pilgrim's Rest: sheltered litter, moss and weeds; clearer southern threshold.
Ruined Crossing: paired bridge posts and dry approach; wet banks remain reedy and
  the deck stays free of generated vegetation. Water/bridge geometry is unchanged.
Widow's Pool: damp soil, irregular shallow/deep water colours, clustered moving
  reeds, and a subtle dry-bank route, not a path painted across the water.
Quarry: gravel-biased ground, light apron and chisel/stone-like edging through the
  existing approach; no new collision shelves or traversal restrictions.
Broken Waystone: a modest worn stone approach into the existing offering pocket.

WHERE TO TUNE IT
data/stillwood_region.tres:
  Each Biome -> Ground Cover sets family weights, density, height, palette,
  bare fraction and movement response. Weighted biome overlap blends art families.
  Scatter -> Batched Ground Cover sets cell size, items per cluster and chunk cap.
  Wind points to the shared weather-motion Resource.

data/environment/stillwood_wind.tres:
  Direction, Strength, Time Scale, Reduced Effects Multiplier.
  No weather simulation was added. These are future weather-control inputs.
data/environment/wind_canopy.tres and wind_undergrowth.tres:
  Cosmetic displacement in metres, frequency and flutter; trunks/colliders stay fixed.
  Tree/prop Wind Profile fields can override the shared defaults per instance.

Landmark scene roots:
  Ground Cover + Cover Bounds Metres select a feathered local ecological override.
  ApproachDressing -> Definition exposes local points, width, surfaces, edging,
  frame positions/styles, cloth/accent colours and seed.
  These approaches are cosmetic: align them with real doors/paths. They cannot
  carve collision, reroute navigation or change the map.

DRAG-AND-DROP ASSETS
scenes/vegetation/: fern_bed, moss_patch, leaf_litter, fallen_branches, gravel_patch,
  reedy_bank, meadow_patch, woodland_floor (all .tscn). Each is one decorative mesh;
  change its profile, radius, item count or seed in the Inspector.
scenes/props/approach_*.tscn: twelve editable entrance/approach presets, including
  the camp and inner hall. Make a Resource unique before changing only one copy.
scenes/props/ground_cover_zone.tscn: a local ecological override with optional
  editor bounds and bare-area rectangles. Rebuild terrain after moving/tuning it.
Existing trees, bushes, grass and reed prefabs inherit the wind integration.

EDITOR WORKFLOW
If the project was open during this pass, reopen it once so the new shader-global
and autoload settings are registered by the editor as well as the next game run.
All new geometry is visible in the 2D editor, including the existing local terrain
preview and distant overview. Use Rebuild terrain after editing biome/zone data.
Preview and runtime share the same profiles, world-space seeds and geometry code.
Generated meshes remain unowned/disposable; manual scene instances are not erased.
Wind uses runtime gameplay time; editor previews intentionally show a static pose
instead of starting gameplay, audio, particles or an extra editor animation loop.
Deleting/rebuilding generated content also removes its owned meshes/materials.

READABILITY AND PERFORMANCE GUARDRAILS
Ground cover is decorative. It creates zero colliders, lights or particle emitters.
One shared material and one additional cached mesh per visited terrain chunk.
Default maximum: 256 decorative items per 16 m chunk, with clustered bare pockets.
Most paths, entrances, decks, solid footprints and initial enemy spaces are sparse
or excluded. Tall cover sits below actors and combat indicators at ground Z=-9.
Tree wind affects canopy art only; foreground reveal is integrated in the same
shader, and Raven's existing above-canopy rule is untouched.
One pausable global wind clock; per-object/world-position phase, gust and speed
variation. No TIME-based animation, per-blade processing or motion blur.
Reduced Effects reduces wind strength to 35%; roots/litter/stone remain static.
Retained chunk/notifier culling, lazy tree/prop artwork and the existing 2.5 ms
region work-queue target. Cover generation advances at most four cells or roughly
0.9 ms per visit; a single cell and final mesh upload are indivisible, so these
are scheduling targets, not hard performance guarantees. Ground-cover allocation
is separate from, and adds nothing to, the existing particle budgets.
