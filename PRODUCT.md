# Ashen Vow — Product contract

A PC-first, magic-first top-down2D action RPG. The user requested the complete
whole-game rebuild roadmap, not a prototype approval gate or a foundation-only stop.

## Confirmed identity

Illustrated gothic fantasy using project-contained SVG artwork, Godot scenes,
procedural geometry and shaders. No external art/audio production dependency.
Eight-direction actors; deliberate Souls-like commitment, movement and stamina.
Magic is primary: catalysts supply unlimited basics within normal spell cycling;
stronger spells use shrine-replenished charges. No FP.

## Play and progression

Keyboard/mouse and controller. Preserve the equipment cross, enemy-only lock-on,
moving attacks and free stamina outside combat. Raven is an out-of-combat traversal
form with safe human landing and no forced airborne reversion.
Fixed-stat owned equipment, per-copy upgrades, attribute levelling, free earned-point
respec, authored discoveries/materials, attuned shrine fast travel and local saves.
Poise, direct effects, buildup statuses and summons stay data-driven.

## Scale and world

128 world units =1metre. Human4/6m/s; Raven8/12m/s. Keep current acceleration and
camera Resources, camp origin, authored regional geography and450m Fort distance.
Day/night/weather start as presentation-only. Target60FPS is a future measured
acceptance requirement, not an established result.

## Durable exclusions

No guarding/parrying expansion, motion blur, renderer change, boss, extra regions,
100newspells, Wolf/feline, randomized loot, crafting economy, merchants, quest
campaign, multiplayer or cloud saves. Preserve existing mobile code without
investing in mobile optimization in this roadmap.

## Delivery truth

REBUILD_ROADMAP.txt in docs records implementation and open acceptance.
Do not run Godot imports, automated tests or runtime tests without explicit user
permission. Current implementation is source-reviewed, not runtime-certified.
DESIGN.md describes actual reusable visual rules, not hypothetical approval.
