ASHEN VOW - LEVEL AUTHORING
Updated 4 October 2026

OPEN THE LEVEL
Open res://scenes/forest_camp.tscn in the 2D editor.
The world-authoring plugin is enabled in project.godot. Its 2D toolbar contains
"Rebuild terrain" and "Roof cutaway". If Godot has not picked up the new plugin,
check Project > Project Settings > Plugins for Ashen Vow World Authoring.

Terrain preview now starts automatically. A lightweight overview covers the
region; nearby ground, vegetation and roadside props use the runtime generation
pipeline. Detail follows the editor view after panning/zooming settles. Far-away
trees/props deliberately use simplified silhouettes, not full-detail thousands
of live nodes. Ground meshes and nearby decorative nodes prepare in batches.
This is a structural level preview, not a live combat/VFX simulation.

The ForestCamp Inspector also exposes:
- Preview Follow Viewport: disable to work with a fixed detail window.
- Preview Center Metres and Preview Half Extent Metres: manual detail window.
- Rebuild Region Preview: regenerate after changing authored placements/data.
The detail window is bounded to 16-64 metres half-extent.

EDIT ENEMIES
Enemies are actual scene instances now, not Marker2D spawn instructions.
Expand Actors > Landmarks; editable children are enabled for the existing kits.
The chapel Sentinel is under:
  chapel > Building > GroundFloor > Occupants > court_sentinel1
Select and move the enemy itself. Roof cutaway is editor-only and exposes the
interior. Local instance overrides remain saved in forest_camp.tscn. Opening a
landmark scene directly edits its reusable source instead.

Drag additional enemies from res://scenes/enemies/:
  court_sentinel.tscn
  grave_warden.tscn
  greywatch_warden.tscn
  training_dummy.tscn
The three wardens use the existing Sentinel AI/stats; they are not new archetypes.
Display Name Override changes a placement label without mutating shared stats.
Place outdoor enemies under Actors or another Y-sorted authored container.
For buildings, place them under the correct BuildingFloor/Occupants, not its
fading Interior artwork group. The actor inherits the floor on startup.
Rebuild terrain after moving/adding an enemy so generated dressing clears its
starting position. These scenes use world navigation and reset to their authored
local positions at shrine rest.

DRAG-AND-DROP ASSET LIBRARY
All paths below are res:// paths. Drag the .tscn, not its implementation .gd.

scenes/landmarks/ - complete editable compositions (existing kits)
  chapel, graveyard, fort, watchtower, quarry, widows_pool, crossing,
  pilgrims_rest, split_oak, waystone

scenes/vegetation/ - reusable individual plants
  broadleaf, broadleaf_old, conifer, conifer_tall, dead_tree,
  bush, thicket, grass_clump, reeds

scenes/props/ - reusable individual props
  campfire, shrine, boulder, rock_small, rubble,
  headstone, leaning_headstone, tomb_slab, waystone,
  low_wall, high_wall, buttress, arch, stairs, bridge_deck, tower,
  fallen_tree, abandoned_tent, lake, pond, dressing_exclusion

Each name above has a .tscn extension. Inspector fields control variations,
footprints, height, collision and tree family/size. Ordinary low props and
trees remain flyable; high walls/towers explicitly block flight.
The campfire in forest_camp is now an instance of its reusable prefab.
Shrine scene colours/layer now match their runtime starting values.

Important kit distinctions:
- props/tower is a solid tower prop; landmarks/watchtower is the complete ruin.
- props/arch is overhead artwork; add separate solid piers/walls around it.
- props/bridge_deck is a walkable visual, not a water-collision eraser. Use
  landmarks/crossing for the existing bridge plus separated water footprints.
- props/stairs is decorative. Logical floor transitions use scenes/buildings/
  and their elevation resources; the art alone does not change storey.
- Lake/pond bodies block humanoids and prevent landing, but permit flight.
- Duplicate landmark kits need a new Landmark ID if they should be separate
  map/discovery destinations. Positions come from their scene transforms.
  Routes do not automatically reroute when a landmark moves.

HAND-PLACED VERSUS GENERATED
Put your own instances under Actors, a landmark, or a building floor.
Do not edit generated preview nodes: they are disposable, unowned, and not saved.
Rebuild replaces only generated content, never owned manual placements.
Generated trees respect authored tree spacing, prop footprints, enemy clearance,
landmark reservations and explicit dressing exclusions.

To replace a generated grove with your own composition, drag in
props/dressing_exclusion.tscn, set Bounds Metres, then click Rebuild terrain.
It reserves dressing space without adding collision or erasing ground/routes.
Place your own trees/props inside it. The reservation also applies at runtime.

Region routes, biome colours/density and scatter settings are authored in
data/stillwood_region.tres. Physical scale remains 128 world units per metre.
Terrain is still resource/procedural authoring, not a newly added terrain brush.

VERIFICATION / LIMITS
Source, scene-reference, typed-container, ownership and diff inspection only.
No Godot launches/import commands, automated tests or runtime tests were run.
Later check editor reopen, panning at wide zoom, drag/drop and undo, saving and
reopening overrides, roof cutaway, generation after exclusions, and runtime
enemy navigation/rest reset. Visual quality and editor/runtime performance have
not been measured. Gameplay particles, audio and AI are not started in preview.

An active editor saved additional scene overrides during this work, including
a moved chapel Sentinel. Those edits were preserved, not reset to old anchors.

ENVIRONMENTAL POLISH ADDITIONS
See ENVIRONMENT_POLISH.txt for the region audit and tuning guide.
New batched vegetation patches live in scenes/vegetation; editable approach
presets and ground_cover_zone.tscn live in scenes/props. Their profiles are in
data/environment. Landmark roots now expose Ground Cover / Cover Bounds Metres.
Wind is shared and pausable; no new collisions or per-blade processing were added.
After editing generated cover/biome/zone settings, click Rebuild terrain.
