ASHEN VOW - DAMAGE INVESTIGATION
4 October 2026. Source inspection only; no combat rebalance applied.

CONCLUSION
There are concrete authoring inconsistencies and presentation behaviours that
can make damage feel inconsistent. I did not establish one reproduced runtime
cause for the reported feel. I found no world-scale multiplier in the inspected
health-damage calculation: scale changes melee/query geometry and recoil, not
the numerical health damage.

PRIORITY FINDINGS

1. Wanderer Sword light/charged definitions are inconsistent.
The live weapon points to data/attacks/presentation/wanderer_sword_light.tres
and wanderer_sword_charged.tres. The charged definition is a Sentinel sweep:
145 physical, no AttributeScaling reference, zero stamina cost, 0.65s windup,
0.12s active, 0.85s recovery. The light definition is 110 physical by default,
22 stamina, 0.10/0.10/0.18s timing, strength 0.025 and dexterity 0.020 per point
above 10. Charging therefore gives only ~32% more base damage with much more
commitment, and no attribute scaling for that charged action.
Recommended next fix: give this weapon an explicitly authored player charged
action. This is a balance decision; it has not been silently changed here.

2. Invalid serialized values in the light action.
Both data/attacks/presentation/wanderer_sword_light.tres and the older
data/attacks/sword_light.tres contain:
  charge_threshold = null
  charged_action_name = null
These fields are typed float and StringName in AttackDefinition. The controller
compares hold time directly against charge_threshold. The exact loader/runtime
outcome has NOT been tested; do not assume a reliable charge threshold from
these files. Recommended repair: explicit valid values (or remove the invalid
overrides to use the declared defaults, currently 0.45 and "charged").

3. Damage numbers are rolling totals, not per-hit labels.
scripts/damage_number.gd resets its total only when the gap since the previous
accepted hit reaches 0.8 seconds. Closely spaced sword hits, beam ticks and burn
ticks accumulate in one label. A growing displayed number is not that much
damage on every tick. Receivers cap the displayed amount at remaining health.

4. The visually armoured Sentinel has zero mitigation.
Its current vitals use data/defences/unarmoured.tres, which leaves all five
damage-negation channels at zero. It has 560 HP and damage_invulnerability 0.
It can therefore accept every valid rapid tick, unlike a receiver with a
post-hit protection window. Armoured-flesh VFX do not provide actual armour.

5. New elevation filtering can reject an otherwise overlapping attack.
Receivers now check Elevation.accepts_hit; spell queries also filter by cast
elevation. Same-floor damage is not numerically reduced by this check. Wrong
floor assignment rejects a hit entirely. Default outside and chapel ground
floor are level 0. New enemy placements must be parented under the intended
BuildingFloor (or use the established explicit elevation component).
Actual stair transitions/failed-hit scenarios remain runtime-unverified.

6. Editing an old resource may not edit the live payload.
Wanderer Sword still declares old sword_light/sentinel_sweep external references,
but its actual light_attack/charged_attack fields use the presentation copies.
For spells, delivery_definition.attack overrides the cast-context attack when
present; otherwise the cast attack is used. Always inspect the assigned live
reference, not a similarly named file.

EXPECTED NUMBERS FROM CURRENT SOURCE
All player attributes 10, target full 560 HP, zero defence, no buffs/imbues,
no rejected hits. These are arithmetic expectations, not runtime test results.

  Wanderer Sword light:      110 physical per accepted hit (6 hits to kill).
  Wanderer Sword charged:    145 physical per accepted hit (4 hits to kill).
  Star Shard:                145 magic per accepted hit (4 hits to kill).
  Prismatic Ray:              50 magic per 0.25s scheduled tick; a fully landed
                              2s channel targets 8 ticks / 400 total.
  Blackflame Shard at 10:    120 fire impact + ~11 HP over 0.75s.
  Blackflame Shard at 60:    120 fire impact + 28 HP over 1.25s.

Blackflame's percentage component is total damage, not percent per tick.
The normal fire hit is mitigated; the resolved percentage burn bypasses defence
but still passes receiver invulnerability checks. Same-ID burns refresh rather
than stack; they also stop when attribution dies. Its separate fire attack has
no attribute scaling reference. No burn tuning was changed by this pass.

ADDITIONAL SOURCE OBSERVATIONS
- Player melee deduplicates targets for the whole committed attack and checks
  the shape, arc and floor. It does not do a world-wall line-of-sight check, so
  a close target across thin geometry may be eligible.
- A target is marked as hit before its receiver accepts/rejects damage; that
  swing does not retry it later in its active phase.
- Mitigation is percentage-by-channel, then attribute multiplier, then unscaled
  imbue bonus and one final integer rounding. There is no flat armour subtraction.
- Percentage burns schedule small integer amounts at roughly 0.05s intervals,
  retaining rounding remainder on refresh; small totals will have zero/one-HP
  visual steps. Rapid feedback is deliberately restrained, separate from HP.
- These are pre-existing behaviours, not changes made during the editor pass.

FOLLOW-UP PRIORITY
First repair the invalid light-attack fields and decide the intended player
charged-sword profile. Then reproduce one light hit, one charged hit, a complete
beam and Blackflame against the same unarmoured enemy, watching actual HP delta
rather than the rolling label. Check same-floor versus cross-floor rejection
separately. Running that review requires explicit permission under the project's
standing no-tests/no-Godot-launch instruction.
