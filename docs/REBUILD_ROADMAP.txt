IMPLEMENTED SYSTEM MAP
A Architecture
  Resource definitions remain immutable. CharacterState/EquipmentInstance own
  persistent state; SpellCastContext/HitRequest/HitResult own contact context.
  PlayerSpellbook, CharacterProgression, GameSession, ActorStatuses, EnemyMind,
  ActorRegistry, UIFlow and presentation helpers reduce controller responsibilities.
  Existing player public methods remain compatible; movement and committed
  action phase transitions intentionally remain in PlayerController.
  ContentAudit + editor diagnostics inspect IDs, scenes/sockets, nested payloads.
  Native JSON is validated before typed runtime construction; no scene-object IDs
  or executable .tres files are loaded from a save.
B Combat
  All weapon/cast assignments are inventoried in LIVE_TUNING.json.
  Hit eligibility, floor/LOS, receiver acceptance, damage/effects, attribution and
  feedback no longer infer success from a health comparison. Receiver-specific
  poise/hurt/death behavior remains local behind the contract.
  Utilities spend on acceptance, restore at release and recover; interruption
  does not refund. Temporary effects and imbues clear at lifecycle boundaries.
  Development trace describes base, defence, scaling, upgrade and exact payload.
C Magic
  Glimmer (wand/staff) and Votive Spark (seal) are catalyst-owned virtual entries.
  Same basic from both hands deduplicates; no memory; uses=-1 means infinity.
  Finite spell pool survives cycling/removal/re-preparation.
  Existing input-owned channels, child attribution, scheduled ticks, geometry,
  targets, manual B orbiters and per-weapon imbues remain connected.
  Actor-owned status meters add decay/resistance/immunity/retrigger/cleanse;
  poison, bleed and frost have Resources/icons/VFX. Frost is a reusable template,
  not assigned to a new player spell. Blackflame remains a separate direct burn.
D Enemies
  Court Sentinel remains elite. Grave Warden: slow mace/poison; Greywatch Warden:
  spear spacing; Thorn Stalker: fast dagger/bleed; Ash Cantor: ranged fire.
  Perception/moves are Inspector Resources. Distant accepted damage provokes,
  local alerts are bounded, lost sight searches last-known location then returns.
  Windup tracking ends at the move's authored fraction. Existing strike visuals,
  collision timing and weapon-size boundaries remain separate from art.
E Raven
  Out-of-combat entry, moving transitions/sprint, landing checks, collision restore,
  canopy handling and optional camera behavior retained. SVG parts dissolve with
  polygons through the shared surface interface. Combat never forces landing.
F Persistence/progression
  Three character slots; no silent overwrite when slots are occupied. Health,
  stamina, Embers, attributes, owned IDs/upgrades, loadouts/remaining uses,
  memories, reward flags, shrine attunement, map and climate persist.
  Invalid/airborne restores use recorded valid ground/checkpoint, not clipping.
  Zero-Embers death replaces only the player's recovery. World drops persist
  independently. Respawn/rest reset ordinary encounter defeats, not first rewards.
  Shrine services offer levels, respec, equipment upgrades, utilities and travel.
  Development catalogue is explicit; combat lab saves nothing to a character slot.
G Art
  Project-contained SVG source; imported textures are reused, not generated every
  frame. Eight views and six animated body parts for humanoid rigs; sockets drive
  held items. Tree families, weathered props, masonry, cliff strata, roof courses,
  fire/shrine, Raven and training effigy now use illustrated source assets.
  Terrain, water ripples, architecture footprints, paths, foliage meshes, cloth and
  VFX deliberately retain procedural geometry where it serves deformation/scale.
  Existing drag-and-drop scene transforms/colliders/manual placement are retained.
H VFX/audio
  Shared setup/update/stop visual contract and safe detached tails; no gameplay
  work depends on an emitter budget. Body flash/dissolve don't replace reveal.
  Persistent vignette stays independent of configured impact presentation.
  Sound Resources cache synthesized samples;18 prioritized transient voices.
  Sparse pattern music crossfades exploration/combat; six music voices. Audio
  includes floor/interior attenuation; volume, shake, blood and vibration persist.
I World/navigation/climate
  Shared spatial actor index, floor-aware elevation/body lookup, floor/clearance
  local windows and coarse route/stair graph. Local nav capped256 probes/frame,
  eight resident/pending windows. Invalid/pending paths wait rather than teleport.
  Ground-detail preparation capped2 jobs/2.5ms scheduling budget per frame;
  cosmetic terrain cache128 chunks by default; no collision eviction.
  Editable24-minute day, clear/overcast/rain, blended wind/light/ambience,
  interior shelter, paused simulation and no offline clock.
J Map/travel
  Fullscreen M map, mouse zoom/pan, markers/beacons, arrival fade and vignette

  Marker floor and discoveries save; upper-floor placement requires floor support.
  Additional checkpoints at chapel, crossing, Greywatch. Attunement required;
  travel rejects combat/actions/Raven, saves first, prepares destination and validates.
K UI/onboarding
  Inspector Theme asset and reusable settings scene. Equipment cross retained;

  Scrollable loadout/services/settings; owned-item search, controller focus,

  Binding labels refresh immediately; UIFlow prevents modal unpause/input leakage.
  Contextual first-use guidance persists as tutorial flags.
L Authoring/toolchain
  Existing deterministic preview/runtime pipeline retained. Editor asset shelf,

LIMITS THAT MUST NOT BE MISREPRESENTED
- This is an untested implementation, not a release-ready certification.
- Future phase-7 fixes depend on actual parser/runtime/render/profiler evidence.
- The theme's fallback body font and some older menu headings are retained.
  They are not an approved custom display type system; see DESIGN.md review notes.
- Coarse routes guide local collision paths; they do not guarantee reachability.
- Initial world collision remains present across the region; cosmetic caching is
  not full streaming or a measured cure for every load/fullscreen stall.
- Enemy roles start with one configured move each plus the retained elite; no boss.
- No save existed before this roadmap. Schema1 is the first persistent format;
  migration notes document additive fields and refusal of unsupported versions.
